## Yadesh copy rules

- Write to a person in a real spiritual or practical situation, not to an abstract audience.
- Prefer concrete descriptions over polished phrases such as "go deeper", "meaningful", "timeless", "journey", and "framework" when they add no information.
- Do not promise transformation, breakthrough, healing, emotional outcomes, or lasting habits. Describe the reading, the source, and the choice the reader can make.
- Keep Premium claims accurate: the full reading library is free; Premium adds 21-day guided series and unlimited bookmarks.
- Attribute testimonies, miracles, and historical claims. Use language such as "reported", "according to", and "source information" when independent verification is not established.
- Avoid SaaS conversion language such as "unlock", "all-access", "zero risk", "instant activation", and "bank-grade".
- Keep copy ecumenically clear when a series reflects a particular charismatic, Word of Faith, or other Christian tradition.
- Use direct CTAs such as "Read the summary", "See Premium plans", "Start your reading", and "Save this reading".
